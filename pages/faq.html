The machine

Why is there no disk multiplexor block?

MIT built one, board type LG684, and its work is electrical: it fans the controller’s one read and write path out to eight drives. Here a drive is a file on the card, so there is nothing for such a board to do, and the controller selects among eight units by DA<30:28> as MIT’s does.

Rests on MIT’s mit/cadrdc/dm.txt, dm.wls, dm.eco and disk.hand; muir’s src/cable.rs and src/disk_controller.rs; docs/disk-controller.md. The whole answer.

Why is muir the reference, and what does “held tick for tick” mean?

A machine with no reference is a machine nobody can check. Each file in rtl/machine/ is compared against a muir type over a recorded trace, cycle for cycle, and muir.commit names the muir the traces were taken from.

Rests on README.md; the header of mutations/list.txt. The whole answer.

Why is a tick ten nanoseconds when MIT’s grid is five?

TICK_NS is five because that is MIT’s grid, and how long a tick lasts is the board’s business. Every count is MIT’s own, so the machine runs at half the hardware’s speed with every ratio intact; the reason is timing closure.

Rests on the header of rtl/machine/cadr_phase_gen.sv; README.md. The whole answer.

Why does the machine’s own clock disagree with the wall?

The tick is twice as long and the tick counts are untouched, so a CADR wall clock loses half a day in a day and the vertical interrupt arrives at 32.35 Hz. Both are left agreeing with muir, and undoing either is one constant.

Rests on docs/io-board.md, docs/tv.md, docs/board.md. The whole answer.

Is the color TV four bits a pixel or eight?

Four, and nothing here implements eight: a pixel is a four-bit address into a map of sixteen colors, each of three eight-bit channels.

Rests on docs/tv.md; muir’s src/tv.rs; MIT’s sys/window/color.lisp, sys/ucadr/uc-hacks.lisp and cadrtv/lmtv.order. The whole answer.

Why does the screen keep the previous boot’s picture?

Because the real machine did: no reset clears the frame buffer, and MIT wrote the clear in software, which runs when the band comes up.

Rests on docs/tv.md; MIT’s sys/sys/ltop.lisp in the System 100 release. The whole answer.

The boards, and the way they are checked

Why is the debug cable on one Pmod connector?

A board is a debugger or a debuggee and never both at once, and neither group of four pins is ever driven from both ends, so one connector carries the whole link both ways.

Rests on docs/debug-cable.md. The whole answer.

How does a board become the debugger?

With --debug-cable-connect in fpgarc at boot, or cadr-console debug-cable-connect at any time. With nothing said, a board is a debuggee.

Rests on docs/debug-cable.md. The whole answer.

Why are the checks themselves mutation-tested?

make check says the checks pass. make mutants says the checks can still fail.

Rests on docs/mutations.md. The whole answer.

Why is there no ILA or other Vivado debug core?

The free BASIC tier refuses create_debug_core, so the probe is a BSCANE2 and a shift register, which fills from reset and freezes with nobody at the board.

Rests on the header of rtl/plumbing/xilinx7/cadr_probe.sv; docs/toolchain.md. The whole answer.

What license is this under, and what in it is not this project’s work?

The GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later, apart from eight files compiled into U-Boot under the GPL, version 2 or later. The third-party material is on the front page.

Rests on docs/license.md. The whole answer.
